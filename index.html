💕 About Us

Welcome to Got Cans! We’re two college-bound girls on a mission to turn your returnables into something meaningful! If the dirty job of taking back bottles and cans doesn’t happen around your house... let us take it off your hands.

🌟 What We Do: We pick up your dirty, recyclable cans right from your doorstep, no sorting or cleaning required! We collect your returnables, handle all the recycling, and give 10% of every collection to a charity of our choice. The rest helps fund our college goals

We make recycling easy and meaningful, one bag at a time. Every pickup helps the planet, our community, and a good cause. It’s more than just cans, it’s doing good together.
